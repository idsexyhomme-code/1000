Privacy Policy

WorkRadar · effective 2026-06-27

WorkRadar is a free, directional reference tool. We collect as little as possible and never sell your data. Questions or deletion requests: idsexyhomme@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

  • Quiz answers (your selected job and tasks, experience, AI-tool use) — to compute your result. Stored without your name unless you provide an email.
  • Email address — only if you opt in to the weekly signal. Used solely to send that email.
  • Basic analytics — aggregate page visits via GoatCounter, which is cookieless and does not track individuals or store personal identifiers.

2. Why we use it

  • To generate and show your AI-exposure result.
  • To send the weekly signal you requested (email only).
  • To understand, in aggregate, which content is useful — never to profile individuals.

3. Legal basis & automated decisions

Processing is based on your consent (the opt-in checkbox). The score is a directional reference indicator, not an automated decision about you, your employment, or any individual — and must not be used as one.

4. Retention

Quiz answers used for an anonymous result are not tied to you. If you subscribe, we keep your email until you unsubscribe or request deletion, after which it is removed promptly.

5. Sharing & third parties

We do not sell or rent your data. Limited processors may be used to operate the service (e.g. email delivery, hosting, cookieless analytics). We share data only as needed to provide the service or where required by law.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and you can unsubscribe at any time. To exercise any right, email idsexyhomme@gmail.com — there is no disadvantage for doing so.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above reflects the latest version.
