## 0. 재사용 자산 (아무 폼에나 복붙)

| 필드 | 값 |
|---|---|
| **이름** | WorkRadar |
| **한 줄(≤60자)** | See which of your tasks AI is coming for. |
| **태그라인(≤100자)** | A 1-minute test that shows which of *your* tasks AI is coming for — with sources, not hype. |
| **카테고리/태그** | AI, Career, Productivity, Future of Work, Quiz/Test, Free Tool |
| **가격** | Free (pilot $29, waitlist) |

**짧은 설명 (~150자)**
> WorkRadar is a free 1-minute test that shows which parts of your job AI is most likely to automate — task by task, cross-referenced with public AI-exposure data. Honest and source-backed, not hype.

**긴 설명 (~300자)**
> Most "will AI take my job?" content is fear-bait. WorkRadar takes the opposite approach: a fixed, published method that scores your work task-by-task, shows the most- and least-exposed parts, and links its sources. Every score is labeled as a directional reference (not a prediction). Free 1-minute test, plus per-role breakdowns for 50+ jobs (nurses, teachers, developers, accountants…).

## 3. Tier 3 — 커뮤니티 (스팸 금지! *가치 먼저*)

⚠️ 커뮤니티는 노골적 홍보 = 삭제/밴. 반드시 **질문·논의·데이터 공유** 형태로.

### Hacker News (Show HN)
```
Show HN: WorkRadar – a task-level "will AI replace my job?" test, with sources

I got tired of fear-bait "AI will take X jobs" headlines, so I built the
opposite: a fixed, published method that scores a job task-by-task and links
its sources. Every number is labeled a directional reference, not a prediction.
It covers 50+ roles. Honest about what it can't do (scores are hand-estimated,
not a calibrated model). Feedback on the method welcome.
```
→ news.ycombinator.com/submit (제목에 URL, 본문은 위 코멘트)

### Reddit (해당 서브에서 *논의* 유발, 링크는 댓글/자연스럽게)
- r/artificial, r/Futurology, r/cscareerquestions, r/careerguidance
- 형식: "I built a task-level AI-exposure test for 50+ jobs — here's what the data says about [직업]. Curious if it matches your experience." (결과 스샷 + 링크)
- 각 서브 **자기홍보 규칙 먼저 확인**. 일부는 주말/특정요일만 허용.

### 기타
- **Quora / 관련 질문**: "Will AI replace [job]?" 질문에 성실히 답 + 근거로 해당 페이지 링크.
- **LinkedIn 글**: 직업별 결과를 인용한 짧은 인사이트 글 (해당 랜딩페이지 링크).
