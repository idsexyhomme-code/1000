Will AI replace game designers? 🎮

A task-by-task look at how exposed game designer work is to AI — estimated AI-pressure 50/100 (Cloudy). Not a prediction. A directional reference from a fixed, published method.

The short answer. Not wholesale — at least not in the near term. On a task-level view, game designer work carries an estimated AI-pressure of 50/100 (cloudy). The parts under the most automation pressure are level boilerplate, balancing tables, quest text. The parts that anchor the role — hardest for AI to take — are core game feel, systems design. So the realistic near-term story is task reshuffling, not job deletion: the routine slices shrink, the judgment slices become the job.
