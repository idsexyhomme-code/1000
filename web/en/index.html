Will AI take my job?

WorkRadar is a free, task-level AI job-risk check. It scores which parts of your work AI is coming for — with a transparent, published method, and honest about being a directional reference, not a prediction.

Will AI take my job?

For most jobs, not wholesale in the near term. A job is a bundle of tasks, and AI hits some hard while barely touching others. The realistic near-term story is task reshuffling: routine slices shrink, judgment and human slices become the job. The free test shows which of your tasks are most exposed.

How do I know if my job is at risk from AI?

Look at the task level, not the job title. The 1-minute test scores your exact task mix and shows the most- and least-exposed parts, with sources. You can also browse breakdowns for 100+ roles below.

Which jobs are most at risk from AI?

Roles built mostly on repeatable, well-specified work rank highest: data entry, transcription, customer service, proofreading and bookkeeping. See the full jobs most at risk from AI.

Which jobs are safest from AI?

Hands-on trades, physical care and live human judgment are the most resilient — electricians, plumbers, welders, nurses, therapists and chefs. No job is fully immune, but these have the least task exposure. See the safest jobs from AI.

Is WorkRadar a prediction?

No. Scores are hand-estimated, uncalibrated directional references built from public AI signals with a fixed method — not probabilities, and not a verdict on any person or company. See the methodology.
