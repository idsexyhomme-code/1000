The problem

AI-job content is mostly fear-bait: scary, generic, and it never shows its work. "Which jobs will AI replace?" lists don't tell you what happens to your week. So the anxiety has nowhere to go.

What WorkRadar does

It breaks your job into its actual tasks and estimates how exposed each one is to today's AI — then shows the most- and least-exposed parts, and a grounded next move. It's cross-referenced with public AI-exposure research, and it's honest about being a directional reference.

🔍 Task-level, not clickbait

A job is a bundle of tasks. AI hits some hard and barely touches others — so we score the tasks, not the title.

🔗 Sources shown

Anchored to public AI-exposure research (AIOE, Felten/Raj/Seamans 2021) where available — labeled, so you see what has external grounding.

🧭 A path, not just a verdict

Beyond the score: what to defend, what to pivot to, and the tasks that are hardest to automate — your moat.

Honest by design

Scores are hand-estimated directional references — not predictions. No fake precision, no doom-mongering. High exposure means task reshuffling, not job deletion. We'd rather ship a transparent, arguable frame than fear-bait.
