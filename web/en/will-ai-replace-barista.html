Will AI replace baristas? ☕

A task-by-task look at how exposed barista work is to AI — estimated AI-pressure 26/100 (Partly cloudy). Not a prediction. A directional reference from a fixed, published method.

The short answer. Not wholesale — at least not in the near term. On a task-level view, barista work carries an estimated AI-pressure of 26/100 (partly cloudy). The parts under the most automation pressure are order taking, inventory, scheduling. The parts that anchor the role — hardest for AI to take — are hands-on craft, customer warmth. So the realistic near-term story is task reshuffling, not job deletion: the routine slices shrink, the judgment slices become the job.
