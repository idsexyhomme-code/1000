Will AI replace pharmacists? 💊

A task-by-task look at how exposed pharmacist work is to AI — estimated AI-pressure 48/100 (Cloudy). Not a prediction. A directional reference from a fixed, published method.

The short answer. Not wholesale — at least not in the near term. On a task-level view, pharmacist work carries an estimated AI-pressure of 48/100 (cloudy). The parts under the most automation pressure are dispensing checks, interaction screening, records. The parts that anchor the role — hardest for AI to take — are patient counseling, clinical judgment. So the realistic near-term story is task reshuffling, not job deletion: the routine slices shrink, the judgment slices become the job.
